Company: Mou-Hau Logic / 無厚理則科技有限公司.

user-selected B artwork, then cleaned for scalable use. The source selection and
contour geometry are recorded in the company asset manifest. These are artwork
outlines, not a font program or a claim of a newly authored redistributable typeface.
